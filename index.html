Getting started

What is USDC?

USDC is a regulated digital dollar issued by Circle and designed to hold a steady value of one US dollar. Unlike most crypto assets, its balance does not fluctuate against the dollar.

Do I need crypto experience to use Stavera?

No. You connect a wallet, enter an amount and an address, and confirm. The experience is similar to a banking app, and every step is shown clearly before you approve it.

Which assets and networks are supported?

Stavera supports USDC, EURC and cirBTC on Arc Testnet. You can send, receive and swap between all three. Bridging is currently available for USDC to other test networks; EURC and cirBTC bridging are planned.

Payments & fees

How quickly do payments settle?

Arc provides deterministic finality in under a second. Once a transfer is confirmed, it is final and cannot be reversed or reorganised.

What do network fees cost?

Fees on Arc are paid in USDC, so you do not need to hold a separate gas token. Your wallet displays the exact fee before you confirm each transaction.

Can I verify a transaction myself?

Yes. Every transfer returns a transaction link to the Arc block explorer, where its status, amount and fee are publicly visible.

Security & testnet

Does Stavera hold or have access to my funds?

No. Stavera is non-custodial. Your keys stay in your wallet, every transaction is signed by you in MetaMask, and Stavera never has the ability to move funds on your behalf.

Is this real money?

No. This version runs on Arc Testnet, where tokens have no monetary value. You can obtain test USDC from the Circle faucet. Mainnet support will be announced separately.

Which wallets can I use?

MetaMask is supported today. When you click Connect wallet, Stavera prompts MetaMask to add and switch to Arc Testnet automatically, so no manual network setup is required.
